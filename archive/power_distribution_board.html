Power Distribution Protoboard

Battery input enters from the top edge. All three outputs — two XT30 for the LED circuits, one JST for the ESP32 — sit in a row right at the bottom edge, ready to route straight off the board.
Raw +7.4V (unregulated)
Regulated +5V (per branch, isolated)
Common GND
Layout logic: power enters from the top, flows down through the fuse onto the raw bus, branches into the three regulators, and exits as three separate, edge-mounted connectors along the bottom — a straight top-to-bottom flow with the outputs positioned right where you'd route cables off the board toward the main protoboard. As before, the three +5V outputs are electrically isolated from each other — only GND ties them together — and the battery's balance lead never touches this board, only its main power lead does.
