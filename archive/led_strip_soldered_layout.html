Every wire here is ordinary insulated hookup wire, soldered only on the component/top side — no back-side soldering needed. The red +5V jumpers physically pass over the black GND bus (see the "hop" and the side-view inset), but the wire's own insulation keeps them from touching. This is standard practice on single-sided perfboard: whichever line has to cross another gets insulated wire, and the rest of the layout — bus rails, straight Data/Clock drops — stays exactly as clean as the schematic version.
