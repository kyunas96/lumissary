Main Board Layout — Placement Optimized for the Perpendicular Power Board

Top-down view. The power board mounts at 90° against one edge. Everything on the main board is arranged so the noisiest, highest-current connections land closest to that edge, and the antenna sits as far from it as the board allows.

The key move: both JST/level-shifter clusters sit right at the edge closest to the power board — that's where your two highest-current, shortest-necessary wires land, keeping those runs short and out of the antenna's way entirely. The ESP32 module is placed so its connector/header end (5V in, GPIO to the level shifters) faces the near edge, and its antenna end faces the far edge — using the module's own length as extra separation on top of the board's own width. The low-current ESP32 power wire still enters from the near edge (matching where the power board's third output already is) and routes along that same side rather than cutting across toward the antenna.
This assumes your ESP32 module's antenna is at the opposite end from its power/header connections, which is true for most dev boards but worth confirming against your specific Makerfabs board before finalizing hole placement. The keep-out circle is illustrative — use the datasheet's real dimension once you've confirmed the antenna's exact position on your board.
