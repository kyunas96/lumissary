ESP32 → 3× APA102-style LED Strip Wiring Layout

Power/Ground share a common bus rail. Data/Clock stay in private vertical lanes down to the ESP32 — nothing crosses.
+5V bus (to all 3 strips)
GND bus (to all 3 strips + ESP32)
Data line (unique per strip)
Clock line (unique per strip)
Power and Ground pins tap upward into shared horizontal bus rails — one solder run each, no jumpers per connector. Data and Clock pins drop straight down in their own lane to a dedicated ESP32 GPIO pair. Because connector order (left→right) matches GPIO pin order (left→right), no wire ever needs to cross another. The small gap in the GND rail is just where the +5V stub visually passes over it on its way to the top rail — they are not electrically connected there.
