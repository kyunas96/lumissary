Alternate Layout: Power/Ground Flanking, JST Rotated 90°

Connectors now sit the way a real 4-pin JST actually mounts — pins in a single horizontal row (P, D, C, G left to right). P taps straight up, G taps straight down, and D/C still hop the GND rail in between.
+5V — top rail
GND — bottom rail (in the way)
Data — must hop
Clock — must hop
What changed: the connector housing is now a normal horizontal 4-pin JST footprint (P, D, C, G left to right), matching how these connectors actually mount. P still lands on the leftmost pin and taps straight up to +5V; G is now the rightmost pin and taps straight down to GND. Functionally nothing else changed — D and C are still boxed in between P and G, so they still need to hop the GND bus to reach the ESP32. Rotating the housing fixed the physical realism, not the hop count.
Same option as before still stands if you want zero hops with this exact pin order: put the GND bus + 3 short G-jumpers on the back of the board, and run the 6 Data/Clock lines on the front. P and its bus can live on either face.
